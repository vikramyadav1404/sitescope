What SiteScope checks

  • HTTP errors (4xx, 5xx) and redirect chains
  • noindex tags and X-Robots-Tag headers
  • Missing, short, long or duplicate titles
  • Missing or duplicate meta descriptions
  • Missing or multiple H1 headings
  • Missing canonical tags and lang attribute
  • Images without alt text
  • Thin pages under 200 words
  • Slow server responses
  • Pages more than 3 clicks deep
